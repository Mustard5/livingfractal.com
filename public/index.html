Prototype. This is an early demonstration of the Living Fractal generation engine. Configurations are produced by a language model and checked against the NixOS package and options database, but the validation layer is still being refined. Generated output may contain incorrect option paths, unrecognised package names, or insecure defaults. Do not deploy to production hardware without thorough review. This tool is for exploration and experimentation only.
